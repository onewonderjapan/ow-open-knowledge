# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository exists to share working methods openly. Keep interactions
professional and focused on the work.

Expected:

- Be direct about technical problems and specific about evidence. "This leaks on
  input X, here is the output" is far more useful than "this is broken".
- Assume good faith. Contributors here write in Japanese, Chinese and English, and
  not everyone is writing in their first language. Read for intent, not for phrasing.
- Critique the code, the document or the decision — not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution that falls outside the
  repository's scope (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)); scope decisions are
  not personal judgements.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, or posting real customer data,
  real personal names, or credentials in issues, PRs or discussions. This repository
  is explicitly built around keeping such data out — see `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the
  inclusion boundary.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, code review, and
discussions — and to anyone acting as a representative of the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers via the repository's
**Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** (which supports private reports), or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports in Japanese, Chinese or English are all
fine.

For security vulnerabilities, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports and may edit, hide or delete contributions, or block
accounts, when necessary. Maintainers are held to the same standard as everyone else.
